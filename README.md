# 🏥 HealthNest – Digital Healthcare Safety Platform

**HealthNest** is a digital healthcare platform designed to help users organize and manage important health information in one place.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://healthnest-kc4v.onrender.com  

---

## 📌 About the Project

Managing healthcare information can become difficult when medicines, medical reports, checkups, insurance details, and emergency contacts are stored in different places.

**HealthNest** brings these important healthcare features together into one easy-to-use digital platform.

The goal is to make healthcare information more **organized, accessible, and convenient** for users.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 💊 **Medicine Management** – Manage and track medicines.
- 📄 **Medical Reports** – Store and access medical reports.
- 🤖 **Report Assistant** – Get simplified summaries of medical reports.
- 🩺 **Health Checkups** – Manage health checkup information.
- 🛡️ **Insurance Management** – Keep insurance information organized.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Trusted Contacts** – Manage family and emergency contacts.
- 🚨 **Emergency Support** – Quickly access emergency-related information.
- 👤 **Health Profile** – Maintain personal health information.
- 🎮 **Health Games** – Interactive games for user engagement.
- ⭐ **HealthNest Premium** – Premium subscription plan at ₹399/month.
